Creator NE Website & Mobile App Project

Requirements

Project Vision
Build the largest Creator Discovery and Brand Collaboration Platform for North East India where creators build professional profiles and brands discover, filter and collaborate with authentic creators.

User Roles
Creator, Brand and Admin.

Website Pages
Home, About, Creator Registration, Brand Registration, Find Creators, Categories, Blog, FAQ, Contact, Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, Login and Dashboard.

Homepage
Hero banner with Join as Creator and Hire Creators buttons, Featured Creators, Featured Brands, Categories, How It Works, Why Creator NE, Testimonials, FAQ and Footer.

Creator Registration
Name, Photo, Email, Phone, WhatsApp, State, City, Languages, Category, Social Links, Followers, Average Views, Engagement, Bio, Portfolio, Previous Collaborations, ID Verification, Bank Details(Private), UPI.

Brand Registration
Brand Name, Contact Person, Email, Phone, Website, Industry, Budget, Campaign Goal, Target State and Timeline.

Creator Categories
Travel, Food, Lifestyle, Fashion & Beauty, Photography, Cinematography, Tech, Comedy, Fitness, Music, Culture, Education, Finance, Gaming, Art, Wildlife, Adventure, Business and Others.

Dashboards
Creator Dashboard: Profile, Campaigns, Notifications, Portfolio. Brand Dashboard: Search, Filter,
Shortlist, Campaigns, Payments.

Admin Panel
Approve users, Manage campaigns, Analytics, Export Excel, Email notifications, Website settings.

Future Features
Campaign Marketplace, AI Matching, Chat, Ratings, Payment Gateway, Commission System,Mobile App.

Search Filters
State, City, Category, Followers, Platform, Language, Gender, Verification.

Design
Premium UI, Black Theme, Blue/Purple Gradient, Mobile Responsive, Fast, SEO Friendly.

Tech Stack
React/Next.js, Node.js, PostgreSQL, AWS S3/Cloudinary, AWS/Vercel.

Security
OTP Login, Email Verification, SSL, Admin Approval, Spam Protection.

SEO
Meta Tags, Schema Markup, XML Sitemap, Robots.txt, Google Analytics and Meta Pixel.

Long-Term Goal
Develop an API-first platform so the website and mobile app share one backend and can scale into the leading creator marketplace for North East India.
